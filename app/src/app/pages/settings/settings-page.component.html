Setup Guide

Follow these 4 simple steps to get a free API Key in less than a minute:

  1. 1 Go to Google AI Studio and log in with your Gmail/Google account.
  2. 2 Accept Google's free-tier terms of service (it is 100% free for personal use).
  3. 3 Click the blue "Get API Key" button in the top-left sidebar, then click "Create API Key".
  4. 4 Copy the generated key (starts with AIzaSy), paste it into the field above, and click "Save Key".
Privacy & Security: Your key is stored locally on your machine in the encrypted SQLite database. It is never sent to any server except directly to Google's official Gemini API.
Limits: The free tier supports up to 15 requests per minute and 1,500 requests per day, which is extremely generous and more than enough for normal personal ledger use.
